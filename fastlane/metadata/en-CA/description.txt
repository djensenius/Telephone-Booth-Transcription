Transcriber is the private review client for the Telephone Booth art installation. Sign in to the Operator, inspect messages awaiting attention, and prepare transcription, translation, and moderation results before submitting them.

On compatible devices, Apple Intelligence keeps the AI workflow local:

• Speech Analyzer transcribes message audio on-device.
• Apple Foundation Models draft translations and moderation recommendations.
• Generated work stays a draft until you explicitly submit it.
• Audio downloads are integrity-checked before processing.

The app does not run a local server, accept inbound connections, include ads, or use analytics and third-party tracking.
