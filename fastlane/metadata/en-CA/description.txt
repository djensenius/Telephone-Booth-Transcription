Telephone Booth Transcription turns your Mac or iPhone into a private, OpenAI-compatible transcription and moderation server. Point any OpenAI-style client at it and get speech-to-text, audio and text translation, and content moderation — without sending your audio to anyone else's cloud.

It speaks the same wire format as the OpenAI API, so existing tools work unchanged:

• POST /v1/audio/transcriptions — speech-to-text from an uploaded audio file.
• POST /v1/audio/translations — translate spoken audio into English.
• POST /v1/translations — translate an existing transcript into English.
• POST /v1/moderations — flag hate, harassment, violence, self-harm, and illicit content.

Choose how the work gets done:

• On device with Apple Intelligence — the Speech Analyzer for transcription and Apple Foundation Models for moderation and text translation. Nothing leaves your device.
• Local Whisper server — proxy transcription to faster-whisper-server or any OpenAI-compatible Whisper backend you run.
• Local LLM via LM Studio — route moderation through any chat model you host yourself.

Built for privacy and control:

• Every endpoint is protected by a bearer token kept in the Keychain.
• Every request is logged to a local SQLite database — metadata only, never your audio or text.
• On macOS the app can keep your Mac awake while the server is running.

This is a focused developer and operator tool, originally built for the Telephone-Booth art installation — a restored rotary phone booth where visitors leave messages that the app transcribes and moderates. It has no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.
